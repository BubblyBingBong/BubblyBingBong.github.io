Currently studying electrical engineering and computer science at UC Berkeley. I am interested in robot foundation models, agents for robotics, and efficient ML systems.

I am currently doing research at NVIDIA GEAR on agentic pipelines for GR00T and at the Berkeley AUTOLab on code-as-policies for robot manipulation. Previously, I worked on inference at NVIDIA TensorRT-LLM, evolutionary program search at Google DeepMind, on-device diffusion at Qualcomm, and 3D scene understanding at Matterport.
